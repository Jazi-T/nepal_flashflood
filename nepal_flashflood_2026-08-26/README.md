# Nepal Flash Flood — 26 August 2026

Incident workspace for collecting, verifying, analyzing, and reporting information related to the reported flash-flood event in Nepal on 26 August 2026.

## Verification status

Event details have not yet been independently verified in this folder. Record every factual claim in `01_sources/source_log.csv` and distinguish confirmed information from preliminary reports.

## Folder guide

- `00_admin/` — contacts, scope, and working notes
- `01_sources/` — source register and saved source material
- `02_media/` — photographs, video, audio, and captions
- `03_data/` — raw and processed datasets
- `04_maps/` — GIS files, map exports, and location notes
- `05_analysis/` — chronology, impacts, needs, and verification work
- `06_reports/` — situation reports, briefs, and final outputs
- `07_archive/` — superseded material retained for traceability

## File naming

Use `YYYYMMDD_HHMM_source_description.ext`. Use Nepal Time unless a source specifies another timezone; record that timezone in the source log.

## Handling rules

1. Preserve original files in `03_data/raw/` or the relevant media folder.
2. Never overwrite originals; put cleaned or transformed copies in `03_data/processed/`.
3. Record the source URL, publication time, access time, location, and verification status.
4. Do not publicly share personal data, precise locations of vulnerable people, or unblurred images of casualties.
5. Label estimates, eyewitness reports, and modeled results clearly.
